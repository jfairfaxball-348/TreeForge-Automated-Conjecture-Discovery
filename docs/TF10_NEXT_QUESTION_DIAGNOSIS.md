## Process retrospective

TF1 showed that zero candidates can be a valid controlled outcome. TF2 showed that full-dimensional
hulls can create enormous opaque families. TF3 showed that one-feature ratios improve legibility but
often fit bounded-order scale rather than structure. TF4 showed that fixed pairwise hulls recover
interpretable low-dimensional geometry.

TF5 showed that finite survival plus a narrow exact-form search can still hide stronger published
theory. TF6 showed that awkward scaling is not a reason to delete an invariant with a genuine
structural recurrence. TF7–TF8 showed that facets can expose real mathematics without themselves
graduating. TF9 showed that independently derived mathematics can still be subsumed by stronger
prior art.

The resulting constraint is question-first design: a feature, target, grammar, or domain should
change because a mathematical question requires it, not because the change may produce output.

## Invariant-vocabulary audit

The heavily exercised discovery vocabulary is domination_number against order, leaf_count,
support_vertex_count, max_degree, diameter, matching_number, and
maximal_independent_set_count. It has already been seen under full-dimensional, ratio, and pairwise
grammars. The archived MIS fan is not reopened.

Among core invariants that were present but withheld, edge_count and degree_sum are affine tree
identities; min_degree is constant on the nontrivial discovery universe; independence_number is
redundant with order and matching_number; and radius is determined by diameter. eccentricity_sum,
wiener_index, and cherry_count are natural exact statistics, but TF0–TF9 expose no domination
mechanism that specifically requires one of them. Availability is not enough to promote them.

The optional TreeStack estimator, finite ProbStack probability, and Greedy Uniformity bias are exact
within their documented scopes but are specialized predecessor-inspired quantities. Promoting one
would import a separate programme rather than answer the TF10 diagnosis.

Natural absent parameters include independent domination number, total domination number, and
packing number. Each is legitimate mathematics, but none is selected by a diagnosed TreeForge blind
spot. Vertex-cover number is redundant with matching number on trees. No new invariant is selected.

## Target audit

There is no evidence that domination_number is exhausted as a mathematical target. The accumulated
failures diagnose grammar, scale, and prior-art issues more clearly than target exhaustion. If
TreeForge later resumes, domination remains the default unless an independently posed question
selects something else.

Switching to a target such as wiener_index merely to leave the domination thread would create a new
programme without a precise question. No target change is selected.

## Grammar and subclass audit

The TF2 full-dimensional regime remains rejected. Ratios-only are not a compelling next step after
TF3. Fixed pairwise hulls remain the best interpreted baseline, but rerunning the same design without
a new question is repetition.

A restricted three-feature order/leaf/support grammar is not frozen because no exact missing
three-way mechanism has been identified. Subcubic trees are a standard natural class, but no
TF0–TF9 obstruction identifies maximum degree three as the mechanism of an unresolved question.
Caterpillars and spiders already serve as adversarial/interpretation families. Twin-free, corona,
and fixed leaf/support classes are too entangled with the closed TF7–TF9 thread.
